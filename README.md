## About Me

I like problems that don't stop at the screen — a school's paperwork, a hackathon's judging bottleneck, a physics lab bench with three sensors on it. Most of what I build ends in something you can actually open, log into, or plug in.

- 🎓 **Studying** B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering at **Siddaganga Institute of Technology, Tumakuru** *(2025–2029)*
- 💻 **Building** full-stack products, multi-agent AI systems, and embedded/IoT hardware
- 🧠 **Currently into** LLM agent pipelines, real-time systems, and making hardware talk to good software
- 🎭 **Off-screen** — drone club, street theatre, and far too many ideas at 2 AM
- 📫 **Reach me** at [arunishrajput7@gmail.com](mailto:arunishrajput7@gmail.com)
